## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Automatic tracking
- Detects when your games open and close, in the background.
- **Smart pause**: AFK time (no keyboard/mouse) and minimized games don't count as playtime.
- **Overlay** showing the session time on top of your game — `Ctrl+Shift+O`.
- Works with any game: Steam, Epic, Xbox, Riot, Battle.net or any `.exe`.
- Add games manually, by scanning a folder or straight from your Steam library.

### 📊 Statistics
- Total time, daily average, **daily goal**, longest streak and longest session.
- Progress charts, yearly activity map, day of the week and hour of the day.
- Table with time, last played and session count for every game.
- **Yearly recap** with the numbers of your year.
- Export your history to **CSV**.

### 🏆 Achievements and profile
- Achievements unlocked as you play.
- Profile with avatar, frame, level and inventory.
- **Compare**: put your stats side by side with another player by @nickname.

### 🖥️ PC Monitor
- Real-time CPU, GPU, RAM, temperatures, network, ping and **FPS**.

### 🎨 Make it yours
- Themes, per-page wallpapers, blur and opacity.
- Tray icons, cursors and profile frames.
- **Nixie**, the app's assistant, with lines in Portuguese and Japanese.
- Background music — `Ctrl+Shift+M`.

### ☁️ RuneHub account *(optional)*
- Sync your stats with your [RuneHub](https://runehub.runeprojects.com.br/) profile.
- **Cloud backup** of your history so you never lose anything (or can move it to another PC).
- **Steins Store**: frames, icons, themes, voices and extras with Runes and Mahoos.

### 📱 On your phone
- Scan a **QR Code** and follow the dashboard from your phone on the same Wi-Fi network.

## 🚀 Installation

1. Open the [**latest release**](https://github.com/guilhermealceu/steins-tracker/releases/latest) page.
2. Download **`SteinsTracker-Installer-X.Y.Z.exe`** (recommended installer).
3. Run it and follow the wizard. Done — the app opens by itself at the end.

> **"Windows protected your PC"?** The SmartScreen warning shows up for new apps that don't have a
> digital signature yet. Click **More info → Run anyway**.

**Requirements:** Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). Nothing else to install.

### 🔄 Updates
Steins;Tracker lets you know when a new version is out and updates itself — none of your data is lost.

---

## 🔒 Privacy

- Your history is saved **on your PC**, in `Documents\SteinsTracker\data`.
- Nothing is sent to the internet **unless you connect a RuneHub account** to sync or back up.
- You can use Steins;Tracker **100% offline**.

## ❓ FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Is Steins;Tracker free?</b></summary>

Yes. The app is free. Cosmetic items and optional extras are in the Steins Store, through
[RuneHub](https://runehub.runeprojects.com.br/).
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Will it slow down my games?</b></summary>

It shouldn't. The app only checks running processes every few seconds and spends most of its time idle
in the Windows tray.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>My game wasn't detected. What now?</b></summary>

Open **Select games** and add the game's `.exe`, scan the folder where it's installed or import it
from your Steam library. From then on, time is tracked automatically.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do I need internet?</b></summary>

No. Internet is only used to check for updates and, if you want, to sync with RuneHub.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>I got a new PC. How do I bring my history?</b></summary>

With a RuneHub account, use **cloud backup** and restore it on the new PC. Without an account, copy the
`Documents\SteinsTracker\data` folder to the same place on the other PC.
</details>
